# Privacy Policy

BookMriya is an OPDS client for Android. It does not collect, store, or send any personal data to the developer or to third parties.

- Book catalogs, downloads, and subscriptions are stored only on your device.
- Catalog passwords are encrypted on your device (Android Keystore).
- The app connects only to the OPDS catalogs you choose (Flibusta, CoolLib, unglue.it by default, or your own).
- No analytics, no ads, no tracking.

Contact: via GitHub Issues.
